What is Logiscool?

An international network teaching kids and teens to be creators, not just consumers, of technology.

Logiscool teaches digital literacy, AI and coding to children aged 6 to 18. Lessons are built around projects kids actually want to make, such as games, websites and worlds, so learning feels like play and builds creativity, logical thinking and problem-solving along the way.

The winter camps bring that approach into the school holidays: short, hands-on weeks where your child finishes with something real to show.
